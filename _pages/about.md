# Short Bio

Hi there! This is Libo Yan (延李波). I am currently a Ph.D. student at the School of Artificial Intelligence, Xidian University, and a member of the Key Laboratory of Intelligent Perception and Image Understanding of Ministry of Education, IPIU.

My research interests mainly focus on 3D vision, spatial intelligence, and multimodal understanding. If you are interested in collaborating with me or want to have a chat, please feel free to contact me through e-mail!
